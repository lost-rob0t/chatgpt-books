** Fikes and Nilsson -- STRIPS
Citation: Richard E. Fikes and Nils J. Nilsson, “STRIPS: A New Approach to the Application of Theorem Proving to Problem Solving,” =Artificial Intelligence= 2(3--4), 189--208, 1971. DOI: 10.1016/0004-3702(71)90010-5.
Primary copies/records inspected:
- SRI Technical Note 43 / ADA459687 crawler-accessible report record and abstract.
- original-paper copy indexed by CiteSeerX, headed “October 1970 ... Artificial Intelligence Group ... Technical Note 43”.
- DOI/journal metadata cross-check through bibliographic indexes for the published 1971 article.
Authority: original authors' technical report/article plus DOI journal record.
Relevant material: search over world models; parameterized operator schemata; applicability conditions; add/delete effect descriptions; distinction between problem-solving operator descriptions and external action routines; use of theorem proving and GPS-style means-ends analysis in the historical system.
Affects: Chapter 8's deliberately narrow STRIPS-style representation and the explicit warning not to equate the course-local BFS planner with the complete historical STRIPS algorithm.
