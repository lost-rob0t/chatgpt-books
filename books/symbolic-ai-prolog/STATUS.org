* Active worker draft -- Project 2 milestone C
- Issue #110 specifies Chapter 9 cost-aware and heuristic frontier policy as the next dependency-ready slice after merged relational action-schema planning.
- Chapter 8's =schema/5=, =applicable/3=, =apply_instance/3=, canonical ground state, and plan-provenance contracts remain the transition kernel. Cost changes selection policy rather than silently rewriting action semantics.
- Action costs are explicit closed data =costed(Schema,Cost)=. This milestone accepts non-negative integers only; negative and non-integer costs fail closed before search.
- Uniform-cost search uses current SWI =library(heaps)= for a min-priority frontier and =library(assoc)= for a ground-state best-known-=g= map. Queue keys are ground =priority(F,Sequence)= terms so equal-priority behavior is deterministic for the teaching fixture.
- A strictly cheaper rediscovery updates the best-known cost and enqueues the new path. Equal/worse rediscoveries are reported as =dominated/4=. Older queued paths that later lose to a cheaper route remain in the heap and are reported/skipped as =stale/3= when popped. Strict improvement prevents a zero-cost cycle from repeatedly reinserting an already-known state at equal cost.
- A* reuses the same transition, best-cost, stale-entry, reconstruction, and trace machinery with =F = G + H=. Heuristics enter only as =zero= or a validated finite =table([estimate(State,H),...])=; the planner never executes a caller-provided heuristic goal. Missing, duplicate, negative, or non-integer heuristic data fails closed. No code claim is made that arbitrary tables are admissible or consistent.
- The synthetic weighted technical-recovery fixture makes the objective mismatch executable: one-step remote recovery costs 12, while =shuttle + onsite_restore= is two actions totaling 11. =taxi= first queues the same on-site canonical state at =g=2=, =shuttle= improves it to =g=1=, and =bus= provides an equal-cost dominated alternative.
- Chapter 9 derives the mechanisms, includes worked frontier traces, four failure labs, twelve exercises, theorem/implementation claim boundaries, and editable/rendered =search-strategy-frontiers.dot/svg= assets.
- Research record 06 inspects Dijkstra 1959, Hart/Nilsson/Raphael 1968 plus the authors' 1972 correction, and current official SWI =library(heaps)= / =library(assoc)= documentation. The manuscript keeps historical theorem conditions separate from bounded repository evidence and does not claim benchmark/scaling, PDDL, temporal/probabilistic planning, negative costs, production performance, LLM, neuro-symbolic, or Prolog-RLM behavior.
