* Findings
The manifest exposes ninety persistent and thirty-three transient documents.
The persistent SDK inventory contains =org=, not =organization=. Relation
requires source, destination, and predicate. StarReference requires schema and
id. Shape validation does not consult a backing corpus for endpoint existence.

The SDK uses exact numeric parsing/serialization, and generated decimal fields
remain strings. The pinned parser alone retains the last duplicate object
member. The lab records that limitation and uses a small, separately labeled
book-local pair-inspection guard before the unchanged text reaches the SDK.
This does not claim a product fix or complete parser resource hardening.

One historical synthetic person migrates through the official SDK helper with
original payload retention. Already-current valid input preserves its meaning.
These examples do not establish all historical mappings or full compatibility
fixture coverage.
