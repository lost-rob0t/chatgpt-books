Two requested chapters teach temporal multisighting geolocation and profiling
of the user's own enrolled equipment. All worked data is fictional, and actual
hardware remains unspecified. Dedicated enrolled BLE beacons are an explicit
teaching assumption, not a claim about phones or headphones.

* Primary research
- [[https://www.bluetooth.com/bluetooth-le-primer/][Bluetooth SIG primer]]:
  advertising/scanning are distinct states; advertising is unreliable broadcast;
  identical payload can repeat across advertising channels; timing is not a
  guarantee that every configured transmission appears in a collector.
- [[https://www.bluetooth.com/core-specification-6-feature-overview/][Bluetooth Core 6.0 overview]]:
  RSSI distance estimation depends on reference calibration and environmental
  effects. Receiver coordinates must not be copied onto observed emitters.
- [[https://www.bluetooth.com/download/bluetooth-security-and-privacy-best-practices-guide/][Bluetooth privacy guidance]]:
  address changes make observed radio attributes unsuitable as a universal
  persistent asset identity. The lab uses an explicit operator enrollment map;
  it does not infer links across unknown addresses.
- [[https://developer.android.com/reference/android/bluetooth/le/ScanResult][Android ScanResult]]:
  RSSI is dBm; observation time can be a receiver-local time since boot, not UTC;
  transmit power can be unavailable.
- [[https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/bluetooth/bt-permissions][Android permissions]]:
  collection/permission settings can affect visible beacons. Missingness must
  not be attributed automatically to asset absence.
- [[https://www.bluetooth.com/learn-about-bluetooth/feature-enhancements/channel-sounding/][Channel Sounding]]:
  cooperative ranging is a distinct capability, not supplied by generic RSSI.
- [[https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc5905/][RFC 5905]]:
  clock offset and uncertainty vocabulary. Supplied constant offsets in the
  course data do not implement synchronization or time-of-flight ranging.

* Canonical mapping
The common document envelope and asset/observation/analysis/geo-point/location
declarations were read from current core.star. There is no Bluetooth-specific
observation dtype to invent. Course measurement structures must be documented
payloads or separate wrappers. Decimal/geotype semantics are preserved.
Receiver context, inferred equipment state and profiling results remain
separate records.

* Experiment design
The local model is a conservative west/east zone baseline with supplied
calibration. Clock uncertainty and valid enrollment/receiver-pose intervals
restrict eligible sightings. Replay-like repeated receiver/sample identities
are data deduplication cases, not a cryptographic replay-detection claim.
Repeated evaluations of the same support cannot create new transition evidence.
Unknown/coverage gaps reset continuity rather than generating a path.

Profiles expose raw/accepted/rejected counts, denominators, calibration/test
separation, visibility, abstention and transition semantics. No person
profiling, actual RF capture, device access, new software installation or
product-runtime change is performed.

* Verification boundary
Chapter text, canonical fixtures and lab tests must be checked on the exact
published head. Local execution is not assumed available. Hosted CI owns the
reproducible SDK tests, publication and EPUBCheck result. Rendered SVG review
is separate from XML or EPUB structural validation.

Thresholds, synthetic positions and expected test sequences are course design,
not measured Bluetooth performance or a platform accuracy guarantee.
