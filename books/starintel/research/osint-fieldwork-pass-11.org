* Primary-source findings
- [[https://humanrights.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Berkeley-Protocol.pdf][Berkeley Protocol]], official HRC-hosted PDF, 2022:
  paragraphs 27, 154--155, 160, 167, 177--180, 184 and 193--194.
  Counter-hypotheses, contextual preservation, transformations, attribution
  versus authentication, independence and corroboration are relevant.
  OHCHR's direct PDF failed; the official Berkeley copy opened.
- [[https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/2466433?hl=en][Google Search Help]]:
  quotation marks, site, minus, filetype, before and after operators verified.
  Date operators concern document updates, not proof of event time.
- [[https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/CR-geolocation-20260326/][W3C Geolocation]]:
  acquisition timestamp, cached maximumAge, reported accuracy, permission and
  recipient responsibilities checked. Actual location is not guaranteed.
  The document is a Candidate Recommendation Snapshot, not described here as
  a final 2026 Recommendation.
- [[https://www.w3.org/TR/hr-time-3/][W3C High Resolution Time]]:
  EpochTimeStamp uses milliseconds from the Unix epoch.
- [[https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/][W3C PROV Overview]]:
  provenance vocabulary is used as conceptual background only.
- [[https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc2606/][RFC 2606]]:
  documentation domains keep search examples separate from real sources.
