* Editorial implications

The previous book foundation was correct for its September pinned experiments
but dangerous as front-door education because a new reader could interpret
=0.9.1=/=0.9.0= as the current canonical model.

Pass 07 therefore adds:

1. a front-of-book mental model centered on contracts and evidence flow;
2. an explicit authority/runtime/adoption version matrix;
3. the canonical =0.10.1= document envelope and naming conventions;
4. distinctions among actor target, investigation target, operation and
   mission;
5. an end-to-end evidence loop and debugging ledger;
6. explicit mastery criteria for what it means to grok StarIntel;
7. a working glossary covering the words most likely to be conflated.

The existing pinned runtime chapters remain useful because they teach boundary
reasoning from executable evidence.  They are now introduced as revision-pinned
runtime labs rather than the canonical schema authority.
