* Where the book goes next

The next chapters add links and monitors as first-class failure relationships, then
separate manual protocol mechanics from OTP behaviors. Project 2 converts the service into
an OTP application with =gen_server=. Project 3 introduces real supervision strategy,
restart intensity, queue/admission decisions, and telemetry. Later projects add releases,
distributed Erlang, persistence boundaries, testing, tracing, observability, and
production debugging.

The progression is deliberate: syntax first, then explicit process mechanics, then OTP.
The goal is to understand why the platform's abstractions work well enough to know when
you are using them correctly -- and to recognize when a green process is merely hiding a
bad protocol.
