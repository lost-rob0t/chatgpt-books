* Findings
** Freeze policy
=DOCUMENTED=. Project tests must not mean "whatever GLEIF returns today". GLEIF data is intentionally refreshed; therefore deterministic book tests require committed source bytes, immutable hashes, retrieval metadata, and an explicit refresh procedure. Live API/Golden-Copy retrieval belongs in a maintenance/import step, not ordinary offline tests.
