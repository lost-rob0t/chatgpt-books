* DOCUMENTED-from-source claims
The following appear in Chapters 29--31 attributed to
=cite:@prolog-rlm-source= and are DOCUMENTED from the recorded read-only
inspection of snapshot =51d2530f= (plus the primary RLM paper, Zhang,
Kraska, and Khattab, arXiv:2512.24601, for motivation).  None of them was
executed by this book against the upstream runtime:

- the architectural thesis that the model chooses semantic strategy while
  Prolog owns execution semantics;
- the closed =rlm_plan= vocabulary (=context/3=, =model/4=, =rlm/2=,
  =tool/3=, =parallel/2=, =retry/3=, =checkpoint/1=, =final/1=) with a closed
  expression language, unknown operators rejected during normalization;
- the parse -> normalize -> validate -> preflight -> execute pipeline, with
  capability/binding/static-budget validation separated from preflight and
  execution;
- tool authority (capability list) and tool availability (host registry) as
  separate facts; missing capability yields =capability_denied= even when the
  closure is installed;
- static plan estimation (steps, depth, parallel width, model/tool/context
  counters) compared with the declared budget before execution; nested =rlm/2=
  estimated at one greater depth; =retry/3= multiplied by maximum attempts;
- one shared remaining budget threaded through nested execution -- children
  do not mint a fresh budget; lexical variable scope is restored on child
  return while accounting and append-only observations survive;
- trace lineage recorded during execution, not reconstructed afterward;
- duplicate lexical bindings and unbound plan variables rejected by
  validation; generic duplicate-subproblem detection requires a trusted
  identity scheme and is not claimed;
- the public facade documents provider-backed recursion deeper than one as an
  explicit opt-in experiment.

* Claim policy for the capstone
- Chapters 29--31 remain =:STATUS: DRAFT= until the deterministic lab passes
  in CI on the exact merged head.  A CI run on that head is the execution
  evidence; a local run is recorded context, never a substitute.
- No chapter claim may be promoted from prose inspection alone.
  DOCUMENTED-from-source statements stay labeled as such and are never
  relabeled TESTED by this book unless this book actually executes the
  corresponding upstream behavior.

  hypotheses; Chapter 31 defines the experiment contract they would require.
  Nothing in this pass measures latency, tokens, cost, or accuracy.

* Non-scope
This pass and the capstone slice explicitly do not include:
- live model calls of any kind (no provider keys, no network model traffic);
- benchmarks of any system, upstream or book-owned, and no reproduction of
  results reported by the RLM paper or the upstream repository;
- execution of the upstream =prolog-rlm= runtime or its test suites;
- claims about upstream behavior at revisions newer than the pinned snapshot;

  semantics beyond what Chapter 30 documents, or duplicate-subproblem
