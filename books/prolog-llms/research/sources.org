** Program/interpreter handoff primary sources
- Luyu Gao et al. =PAL: Program-aided Language Models=. arXiv:2211.10435, inspected 2026-09-17. Used for the architecture in which an LLM generates programmatic intermediate reasoning and an interpreter/runtime executes it. All benchmark results remain UPSTREAM-REPORTED.
- Wenhu Chen et al. =Program of Thoughts Prompting: Disentangling Computation from Reasoning for Numerical Reasoning Tasks=. arXiv:2211.12588, inspected 2026-09-17. Used for the model-program/external-computation split. Reported performance remains UPSTREAM-REPORTED.
- Qing Lyu et al. =Faithful Chain-of-Thought Reasoning=. arXiv:2301.13379, inspected 2026-09-17. Used for its two-stage Translation -> symbolic reasoning chain -> deterministic Problem Solving architecture and the distinction between answer faithfulness to the emitted chain versus correctness of the translation. Reported benchmark results remain UPSTREAM-REPORTED.
