* Research policy
Research for this book must support falsifiable teaching claims rather than decorative citations. Prefer official SWI-Prolog documentation and source, standards/specifications, original papers, original implementation repositories, and vendor documentation for current API behavior. Secondary material may provide context but must not silently become the authority for version-sensitive claims.

Every research pass should record:
- precise question or claim under test;
- retrieval date and source revision/version where available;
- what was DOCUMENTED by the source;
- what was actually TESTED in this repository;
- what remains UNTESTED or EXPERIMENTAL;
- conflicts or negative evidence;
- downstream chapters/projects affected.

* Priority queues
** Prolog foundation
Current SWI-Prolog syntax/term model, unification and occurs-check behavior, module semantics, PL-Unit, DCGs, CLP(FD), meta-call/reflection boundaries, tracing/debugging, resource limits, and safe term parsing/serialization.

** LLM integration boundary
Current structured-output/schema mechanisms, tool/function calling, streaming/partial result behavior, cancellation/timeouts, deterministic fixture strategies, provider/version identification, and safe treatment of model output as untrusted data.

** Neuro-symbolic systems
Primary papers and actual implementations for systems that combine neural generation/retrieval with symbolic constraints, proof, program execution, theorem proving, logic programming, or recursive language-model orchestration. Record what each system actually delegates to each component.

** Evaluation
Methods for comparing direct generation, retrieval augmentation, symbolic verification, repair loops, and recursive orchestration without benchmark leakage or post-hoc metric selection. Track exact datasets/prompts/providers/versions for any measured result.
