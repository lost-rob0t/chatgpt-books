* Research policy
Research for this book must support falsifiable teaching claims rather than decorative citations. Prefer official SWI-Prolog documentation and source, standards/specifications, original papers, original implementation repositories, and vendor documentation for current API behavior. Secondary material may provide context but must not silently become the authority for version-sensitive claims.

Every research pass records:
- precise question or claim under test;
- retrieval date and source revision/version where available;
- what was DOCUMENTED by the source;
- what was actually TESTED in this repository;
- what remains UNTESTED or EXPERIMENTAL;
- conflicts or negative evidence;
- downstream chapters/projects affected.

* Priority queues
** Prolog foundation
Project 1 is now merged through the bounded meta-call/reflection capability boundary. Broader meta-interpreters, planning, and classical symbolic-AI algorithms remain in =books/symbolic-ai-prolog= rather than expanding this foundation indefinitely.

** LLM integration boundary
Project 2A is editor-approved content awaiting merge. Issue #129 is a stacked research/manuscript slice that uses the accepted typed-gateway boundary to teach program, constraint, logic, planner, and recursive-runtime handoffs without giving generated text execution authority. Timeouts/cancellation, replay handling, repair loops and live provider adapters remain later milestones.

** Neuro-symbolic systems
Primary papers and actual implementations for systems that combine neural generation/retrieval with symbolic constraints, proof, program execution, theorem proving, logic programming, or recursive language-model orchestration. Record what each system actually delegates to each component and keep paper benchmark results UPSTREAM-REPORTED until independently reproduced.

** Evaluation
Methods for comparing direct generation, retrieval augmentation, symbolic verification, repair loops, and recursive orchestration without benchmark leakage or post-hoc metric selection. Track exact datasets/prompts/providers/versions for any measured result.
