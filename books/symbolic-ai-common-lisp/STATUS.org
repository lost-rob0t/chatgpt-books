* Symbolic Planner milestone A
- The accepted kernel derives a positive-precondition STRIPS-style teaching model from the already-merged Search and Unification projects. It does not import a second search engine or inspect Project 2's substitution representation.
- States are finite canonical sets of ground symbolic facts. Action schemas expose explicit parameters, positive preconditions, add/delete effects, and nonnegative costs. Preconditions are joined by repeated calls to public =UNIFY= under one substitution; names/effects are grounded by public =APPLY-SUBSTITUTION=; Search receives ordinary successor transitions.
- The permanent suite covers set-like state identity, public-boundary rejection of ungrounded world facts, repeated-variable binding consistency, delete/add semantics, fail-before-transition ungrounded effects, a four-action warehouse plan, unreachable-goal termination, UCS action-cost choice, and duplicate-state coalescing.
- The chapter derives schemas -> consistent bindings -> grounded actions -> state transition -> graph search, includes debugging/failure labs and exercises, and explicitly distinguishes the course-local subset from full historical STRIPS, PDDL, production-rule control, and Prolog-native planning.
- Research records Fikes/Nilsson 1971, the 1993 retrospective, and Bylander 1994; complexity results are not converted into benchmark or scaling claims for this Common Lisp implementation.
