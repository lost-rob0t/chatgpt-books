* Pattern Matcher/Unifier milestone A
- The milestone derives directional structural matching before symmetric first-order unification instead of dropping in an opaque framework.
- Variables use the explicit reserved term =(:var NAME)=; ordinary atoms/conses remain the finite acyclic term language for this milestone.
- Matching carries explicit alist bindings and distinguishes literal/shape/repeated-variable failures. Editorial review found and fixed a directionality defect where successful matcher bindings were incorrectly passed through unifier substitution normalization: a literal datum shaped like =(:var NAME)= could therefore be reinterpreted as a project variable. A regression requires variable-shaped datum to remain literal matcher data.
- Unification dereferences variable chains, applies/normalizes substitutions, handles variables on either side, and performs a mandatory occurs check rather than silently adopting rational-tree semantics. Editorial review also added the direct successful-unifier oracle promised by the manuscript: applying one returned substitution to both original terms must yield =EQUAL= terms.
