* Active slice -- Chapters 9--12 calculus foundations
DRAFT / VERIFY in PR #74. This slice derives average change and secants into the derivative limit/local-linear model; derives partial derivatives, gradients, unit-direction directional derivatives, and the chain rule; introduces affine-regression MSE only far enough to derive and independently check its parameter gradient; and deliberately stops before training/optimizer claims.

The executable calculus lab implements forward and central finite differences, coordinate partials, numerical gradients, directional derivatives, affine MSE, and an independently derived analytic affine-MSE gradient. Its strict finite-real contract rejects invalid steps, non-finite/unrepresentable inputs and outputs, zero directions, and represented steps that fail to move a sample. A deliberate =central_difference(abs, 0)= counterexample shows why numerical gradient checks are evidence rather than proofs of differentiability.

Two deterministic accessible teaching figures are generated from the project code: secants approaching the tangent of =x^2= and a numerical gradient crossing level curves of a quadratic bowl.
