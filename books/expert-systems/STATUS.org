* Active worker draft -- Project 1 milestone F
- Issue #115 tracks the bounded explanation/provenance slice. =research/foundations-06.org= inspects Clancey 1983, Aikins 1980, Doyle 1979, and current W3C PROV-DM material. It explicitly treats W3C PROV as comparison vocabulary only; no PROV serialization/conformance claim is made.
- =code/inference-engine/explanation.lisp= is a read-only layer over the accepted =assumption-network=. It does not change matching, agenda order, refraction, truth maintenance, contradiction detection, or firing policy.
- Explanation nodes have deterministic occurrence-sensitive identities for current fact occurrences, base/assumption support sources, current grounded justifications/rule applications, and current contradiction reports.
- Fact explanations preserve exact occurrence stamps, logical content, fact provenance, current support kind, every current grounded alternative justification, production identity/provenance, bindings, and recursive exact support paths.
- Historical justifications are filtered by exact current conclusion/support occurrence identity plus recursive grounding. A stale reason must not silently attach to a later same-ID reassertion.
- Mixed base+derived support is represented as separate source and justification branches rather than collapsed into one narrative.
- =:absent= and =:unsupported= are typed outcomes. The milestone deliberately does not invent why-not or failed-rule explanations from absence.
- Contradiction explanations preserve the declaration's ID/provenance, shared consistency bindings, both exact counterevidence branches, and each side's support kind. A report whose exact pair is no longer current returns =:stale= rather than being rendered as current conflict.
- =explanation-graph-signature= provides a deterministic data-only inspection representation for regression/debugging use; it is not a graph-isomorphism or interchange standard.
- =explanation-tests.lisp= adds regressions for evidence, assumptions, a two-hop service derivation, rule provenance, independent reasons, mixed support, stale historical reason exclusion, absence, deterministic signatures, current contradiction branches, and stale contradiction reports. Exact-head CI has not yet been observed for this draft.
