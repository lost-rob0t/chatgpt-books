- =research/foundations-05.org= records the assumption/consistency research boundary. Doyle 1979 provides assumption/revision lineage; de Kleer 1986 provides ATMS lineage around first-class assumption sets and inconsistent information; current systemd D-Bus Unit documentation grounds the =ActiveState= technical fixture. - =code/inference-engine/assumptions.lisp= layers an =assumption-network= over the accepted =truth-maintained-network= instead of modifying RETE caches or milestone-D justification ownership.
- Explicit assumptions are exact current base-support occurrence stamps. Ordinary base evidence, assumptions, derived facts, and absence are reported separately without treating the categories as confidence rankings.
- Assumption withdrawal delegates to milestone-D support removal. If another justification still grounds the same exact occurrence, the fact survives as derived; otherwise the accepted cascade removes it and newly unsupported dependents.
- =contradiction-spec= objects have stable IDs, left/right matcher patterns, and provenance. Bindings flow from the left pattern into the right pattern, making shared variables the explicit consistency key.
- Current contradiction reports require two distinct current grounded occurrences and preserve both sides as counterevidence with exact stamps, support kinds, shared bindings, specification identity, and specification provenance. The layer does not automatically choose a winner or retract either side.
- The systemd teaching declaration keys =(unit-active-state UNIT STATE EPOCH)= on both =?unit= and =?epoch= so two different observation epochs are not falsely collapsed into simultaneous contradiction. The epoch is course-local observation context, not a systemd property.
- =run-assumption-network= offers =:halt= and =:report-only=. =:halt= checks before every next firing and returns =:contradiction= while keeping the conflicting state and blocked agenda inspectable; =:report-only= continues for debugging/preview while retaining current contradiction reports. Neither mode is a resolution policy.
- =assumptions-tests.lisp= adds planned regressions for duplicate constraint identity, same-epoch conflict, different-epoch near miss, evidence-versus-assumption reporting, withdrawal, mixed assumption+derived support demotion, initial halt, report-only continuation, and a contradiction created by one firing that blocks the newly enabled next firing.
