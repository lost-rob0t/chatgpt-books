Result: milestone C derives a deliberately simplified positive-pattern RETE-like network from the book's own accepted matcher. The complete-rematch agenda remains the executable oracle. Node sharing, negative/existential CEs, truth maintenance, TREAT, persistence, and all performance claims remain deferred.
